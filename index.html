Legacy Phantom vs. UPI-Simple Solana

Side-by-side architectural comparison showing the exact UX pain points in current Solana wallets and how our design resolves each one using UPI principles.

⚠️ Legacy Solana Wallet (Current Flaws)

High Friction
Feature 1: Send
Cryptographic Address Nightmare
Users are forced to paste 44-character strings like 9wFF...7xK2. No avatar, no verified name, and zero indication if the recipient is a real human or an empty drainer.
Feature 2: Signing
Instruction Bytecode Dumps
Approvals show raw program IDs and instructions. 80% of users admit to blind-signing because they cannot understand what token leaves their wallet.
Feature 3: Fees
Incomprehensible Lamports
Fees displayed as 0.000005 SOL or lamports. Non-crypto users have no intuitive mental anchor and fear high hidden bank fees.
Feature 4: Status
Cryptic Panic Error
A vague "Transaction Failed: Error 0x1771" toast leaves users in full panic thinking their ₹25,000 has been debited and permanently lost.
Feature 5: Onboarding
12-Word Seed Phrase Barrier
Forces newcomers to write 12 random English words on physical paper before they even explore the app. Triggers a 72% abandonment rate.

⚡ UPI-Simple Solana (Our Redesign)

Zero Anxiety
Feature 1: Send
Human-First Contact Cards & First-Time Warning
Lookup by .sol handle or contact alias. Includes verified checkmarks, safety rating, a "First-Time Sending" alert, and a 1-tap ₹10 micro-test option.
Feature 2: Signing
Plain-Language Financial Statement & Risk Shield
Crystal clear "You Give / You Get" with exact Rupee conversions and a dynamic tri-state shield (🟢 Safe / 🟡 Caution / 🔴 Drainer Intercept).
Feature 3: Fees
Rupee-Native Micro-Fees (≈ ₹0.08)
Fees prominently shown in Rupees alongside an educational comparison showing Solana is 99.9% cheaper than credit card merchant fees.
Feature 4: Status
Peace-of-Mind Pipeline & Fund Safety Guarantee
Animated 4-step validator pipeline with an unmistakable promise: "🛡️ NO FUNDS MOVED • PRINCIPAL SAFE" (noting standard validator fee of ~₹0.08 applies for compute inclusion), paired with a 1-tap auto-slippage retry.
Feature 5: Onboarding
Progressive Security Ring & 3-Second Passkey
Instant biometric login in 3 seconds. The wallet introduces a gamified "Protect Your Wallet" ring that nudges cloud backup only as balances grow.
