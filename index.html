Deep Dive: The 3 Core Architectural Pillars

Focusing deeply on the three moments that define user safety, comprehension, and trust.

⚠️ Legacy Solana Wallet

Friction & Fear
Pillar 1: Send
Cryptographic Address Nightmare
Users paste 44-character strings like 9wFF...7xK2. No avatar, no verified name, and zero indication if the recipient is a real human or an empty drainer. Once tapped, funds broadcast with zero cancellation buffer.
Pillar 2: Signing
Instruction Bytecode Dumps
Approvals show raw program IDs and instructions without simulated balance delta. Non-crypto users report complete inability to decipher what leaves their wallet, leading to blind approval or panic abandonment.
Pillar 3: Recovery
Cryptic Panic Error & Abstract Fees
A vague "Transaction Failed: Error 0x1771" toast leaves users in full panic thinking their funds vanished. Fees shown in lamports (0.000005 SOL) provide zero Rupee intuition.
Secondary Touch
Seed Phrase Barrier & Raw Explorer Links
Mandatory 12-word seed phrase on initial launch blocks exploration. Explorer URLs look like phishing links when shared with non-crypto recipients.

⚡ UPI-Simple Solana (Our Redesign)

Zero Anxiety
Pillar 1: Send
Human Contacts + 10s Pre-Broadcast Undo Window
Lookup by .sol handle or contact alias with verified badges and a First-Time Recipient warning. Tapping send activates a 10-second client-side buffer pause with an animated countdown ring and 1-tap Cancel.
Pillar 2: Signing
Plain-Language Balance Delta + Rakshak Mode
Crystal clear "You Give / You Get" with exact Rupee conversions (₹24,663 ➔ ₹24,639) and 4 explicit checks passed. Rakshak Mode provides Hindi/Hinglish copy and opt-in voice readout to eliminate signing blindness.
Pillar 3: Recovery
Validator Pipeline, Honest Fee & 1-Tap Retry
Animated 4-step validator pipeline showing optimistic confirmation (~400ms) with honest fee disclosure: "No funds moved. A small network fee (≈ ₹0.08) may apply." Includes 1-tap auto-slippage retry.
Secondary Touch
Biometric Passkey & WhatsApp-Shareable Receipt
3-second biometric login with progressive backup ring nudges. 1-tap WhatsApp receipt card generates clean, verified payment proof without exposing raw hashes.

Pillar 1: Send + 10s Pre-Broadcast Undo Window

Observed Hesitation Resolved
🚨 Problem

44-character cryptographic hashes cause extreme send anxiety. Because blockchains are irreversible, mistakes cannot be retrieved.

🔍 Evidence

Testers spent ~42s repeatedly checking individual characters. Sneha: "Like firing a missile blindfolded into the dark."

💡 Decision

Verified .sol names + avatar + ₹10 test transfer + 10-second client-side buffer pause with an animated countdown ring and Cancel button.

🎯 Result

Send completion dropped to ~11s. All 5 participants cited the 10s cancel buffer as an indispensable safety cushion.

2

Pillar 2: Plain-Language Signing + Rakshak Mode

Full Comprehension
🚨 Problem

Raw bytecode and program IDs lead non-technical users to blind approvals. English-only jargon alienates vernacular Indian users.

🔍 Evidence

Testers could not interpret bytecode instructions. Kabir: "If this stole all my money, I wouldn't even know until it was gone."

💡 Decision

Clear You Give / You Get in Rupees, 4 explicit safety audit checks, Rakshak Mode (Hindi / Hinglish), and opt-in voice readout (🔊 Suniye).

🎯 Result

Zero blind approvals observed; all 5 participants could accurately recite what tokens they were trading and what fee was charged.

3

Pillar 3: Recovery Timeline & Honest Fee Transparency

Clarity on Reversals
🚨 Problem

Cryptic Error 0x1771 causes panic that funds vanished. Displaying fees in lamports gives zero intuitive sense of cost.

🔍 Evidence

Vikram: "When PhonePe fails, it tells me 'Money not debited'. Here it said 'Failed'. My stomach literally dropped."

💡 Decision

4-step validator timeline + honest banner: "No funds moved. A small network fee (≈ ₹0.08) may apply" + 1-tap auto-slippage retry.

🎯 Result

Zero panic observed on simulated failure. Testers immediately understood Sealevel atomic rollback and tapped the 1-tap retry.

✨

Secondary Touches: Passkeys & WhatsApp Receipt

Frictionless Adoption
🚨 Problem

Mandatory 12-word seed phrases on initial launch create immediate drop-off; sharing raw explorer hashes looks suspicious.

🔍 Evidence

Rohan: "I abandon any app that gives 12 words on screen 2. Give me a passkey now, and ask me to backup later."

💡 Decision

3-second biometric passkey login with progressive backup nudges, plus a 1-tap WhatsApp-shareable receipt card.

🎯 Result

Immediate onboarding without paper seed phrase friction; human-readable receipt cards tailored for everyday Indian social sharing.
